The story behind Maui-SEO

Maui-SEO is built and run by Eye To Ad Media, a search marketing agency founded in 2012 by Zachary Tye Wennstedt. We help Maui businesses get found on Google, in the map pack and in AI answers, with websites, ads and social media to match.

Zach didn't start in marketing. He started in sales. He was a sales manager for multi-million-dollar plumbing companies, then managed national remodeling companies and the sales teams that powered them.

That job teaches you something most marketers never learn: a lead is only worth something if it turns into a customer. He spent years on the receiving end of marketing, watching agencies send glossy reports full of impressions and clicks while his team wondered why the phone wasn't ringing.

So in 2012 he started Eye To Ad Media to do it differently. Build websites that convert. Rank for the searches that actually bring customers. Report on calls and revenue, not vanity numbers. And treat every client's business like it was his own, because he knows exactly what it feels like when it's your name on the payroll.

More than a decade later, that's still the whole idea.

What we believe about marketing

These aren't slogans. They're the rules we actually run the business by.

Calls beat clicks

Traffic is nice. Customers pay the bills. Every report we send ties back to calls, forms, bookings and revenue.

No empty promises

No one can honestly guarantee rankings, and we won't. We promise to put your business first, work on it like our own, and bring everything we know.

Earn it every month

If a channel isn't pulling its weight, we tell you and move the money, even if it means you spend less with us.

Real facts only

We don't put claims on your site that a reader can't check, and we don't invent reviews, awards or statistics. Trust is the whole business.

Respect the place

Maui isn't a backdrop. We write about it accurately, spell its names correctly, and approach sensitive topics like Lahaina's recovery with care.

We build what we sell

The best way to know what works in search is to do it yourself, constantly. That's why we build and run our own sites across Hawaiʻi's visitor economy: diving, hotels, beaches, surf lessons and dining. We test ideas on our own properties before we ever recommend them to a client.

It also means we understand the markets you're competing in, because we're competing in them too.

Outside Hawaiʻi, the same approach powers our dedicated site for contractor advertising, a sales training site built from Zach's years running sales teams, and a marketplace of keyword domains for businesses.
